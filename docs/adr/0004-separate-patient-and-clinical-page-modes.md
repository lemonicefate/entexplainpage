# Separate patient education and clinical quick-reference modes

The page system supports both patient-facing education and clinician-facing quick reference because dense lookup material is frequently needed in the clinic and cannot be excluded. Both modes retain the same square canvas and brand frame, but they use separate approved layout catalogs, typography floors, and capacity rules; a fixed mode label prevents dense professional reference material from being mistaken for patient education.
